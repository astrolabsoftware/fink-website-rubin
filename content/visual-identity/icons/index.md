<p style="text-align: center;">These minimalist icons were developed to illustrate the variety of science cases 
  present in within Fink. Each one highlight a class of source, or progenitor, which might generate an alert. You can find most
  of them in the Fink portal whenever a given object has received a classification. Examples of their placement are given 
  in description below each card.</p>
